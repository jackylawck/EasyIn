## 🇬🇧 English

### Overview
**EasyIn** is a lightweight, privacy-first, zero-telemetry Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to help professionals master high-stakes job interviews and written assessments. 

Built with a deterministic, rule-based architecture (Non-AI), EasyIn operates 100% client-side with zero server tracking. It features 40 real-world business scenarios, decoding the hidden logic of hiring managers and providing structured response frameworks from Junior to Senior levels.

### ✨ Key Features
- **🧠 40 High-Impact Business Scenarios:** Covering crisis management, upward communication, vendor negotiations, data-driven decisions, DEI, and technical trade-offs.
- **💥 Hiring Manager OS:** Uncovers the hidden motives and true expectations behind trick interview questions.
- **📈 Junior-to-Senior Frameworks:** Progressive response scripts demonstrating strategic evolution and career maturity.
- **🆘 10s Panic Button:** Instant safe-landing scripts to buy thinking time when facing unexpected questions.
- **🎯 Interactive Challenge Mode:** Test your judgment with randomized A/B comparison scenarios.
- **📂 Category & Single Question Filter:** Seamlessly filter by 11 standardized business domains or practice question-by-question.
- **🔒 Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** Fully PWA-compliant. Works 100% offline with zero cookies, zero analytics, and zero server logging.

### 🛡️ Privacy & Compliance
- **GDPR & Privacy-by-Design:** All interactions and user states remain local to your browser (`localStorage`).
- **Deterministic Architecture:** Static reference matching with zero automated decision-making or AI hallucinations.
- **One-Click Data Purge:** Instantly wipe all local storage, service worker caches, and logs anytime.

## 🇭🇰 繁體中文

### 專案簡介
**面試易 EasyIn** 是一款輕量級、極致注重隱私與零數據追蹤的漸進式 Web 應用程式（PWA）。旨在協助香港及全球職場人士應對高難度的求職面試與商業筆試。

本系統採用確定性（Deterministic）、無 AI 生成的純前端架構，100% 於用戶瀏覽器本機運行。題庫收錄 40 條真實商業實戰情境，獨家拆解考官內心 OS，並提供由 Junior 到 Senior 的思維進階答題腳本。

### ✨ 核心特色
- **🧠 40 條高頻實戰題庫：** 涵蓋危機處理、向上管理、供應商談判、數據決策、DEI 及技術取捨等 11 大商業範疇。
- **💥 考官內心 OS：** 踢爆刁鑽面試問題背後的真實意圖與考官關切點。
- **📈 Junior 至 Senior 三級框架：** 提供由淺入深的應答範本，展示高階思維與大局觀。
- **🆘 10 秒救命掣 (Panic Button)：** 面對突發提問或腦袋空白時，提供優雅爭取思考時間的降落台詞。
- **🎯 考牌 Challenge Mode：** 隨機抽取題目進行 A/B 盲測，快速檢驗個人思維層次。
- **📂 種類篩選與逐題刷題模式：** 支援按 11 大商業大類精準過濾，或開啟單卡卡片模式專注刷題。
- **🔒 零數據留存 (ZDR guarantee)：** 100% 離線 PWA 支援。無後端伺服器、零 Cookie、零 第三方追蹤碼。

### 🛡️ 架構與隱私合規
- **隱私即設計 (Privacy-by-Design)：** 完全符合 GDPR 數據極小化與「被遺忘權」原則，所有操作僅留存於本機。
- **確定性靜態檢索：** 無演算法偏見與 AI 幻覺，不涉及任何自動化個人評估或資格審查。
- **一鍵銷毀數據：** 提供 Data Purge 功能，可隨時徹底銷毀本機快取與紀錄。

- **Service Worker:** Custom Cache-Busting Control (`sw.js`)
